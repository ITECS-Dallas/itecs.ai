One private proposal still contains legacy ISO 27001 and broad SOC 2 wording; it was not changed because proposal content has its own customer, access, and approval workflow. The broader Insights archive also contains older, dated product, price, timing, ROI, privacy, and compliance language outside this audit's conversion-page sample. This release corrects the v1.4 prepaid-expiry contradiction because it is governed by the current pricing catalog. A separate evidence-led archive review should revalidate older articles against current first-party sources without silently rewriting their publication history.
